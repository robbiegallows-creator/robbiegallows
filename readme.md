About the Artist

​Welcome to my digital gallery. As a multi-disciplinary creator, my practice bridges traditional physical craftsmanship with modern digital and artificial intelligence workflows. Working primarily with physical media like canvas and wood, I specialize in an expressionist and gothic aesthetic characterized by dark themes and spectral imagery.

​By integrating traditional painting techniques with original photography, custom AI multimedia tools, and original digital soundtracks, this portfolio explores the intersection of classic fine art and contemporary digital storytelling.
