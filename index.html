The Hybrid Process

01
Digital Conception

Composition & Chiaroscuro

Developing dark surrealist imagery, atmospheric chiaroscuro lighting, and gothic architecture digitally—building complex spatial layers and narrative depth.

02
Archival Transfer

Canvas Substrate

Transferring high-resolution digital compositions onto premium gallery-wrapped canvas, creating a permanent archival foundation for physical intervention.

03
Physical Overpainting

Impasto Oil & Mod Podge

Applying heavy oil paint and Mod Podge directly over the canvas surface, sculpting physical relief, tactile brushwork, and rich impasto texture.

Artist Statement

Robbie Gallows

Working at the intersection of digital chiaroscuro and tactile physical oil painting, Robbie Gallows creates museum-quality gothic compositions exploring memory, rebellion, and loss.

By overpainting digital canvas foundations with heavy impasto oils and structural Mod Podge layers, each piece bridges raw physical texture with dark cinematic atmosphere.
