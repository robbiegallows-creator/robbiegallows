Process & Environment

In Studio

Tactile & Hybrid Workflow

Where Physical Meets Digital

"Every canvas starts with a digital foundation, printed onto canvas before being heavily worked over with physical oil paints and Mod Podge to construct tactile, dimensional textures."

This hands-on impasto technique creates a tangible surface depth that grounds the atmospheric and dark, chiaroscuro themes of each piece in the physical realm.

Read Studio Updates on Substack
Material Depth

Scale and Presence

"Working at scale allows the physical textures of the medium to engage directly with the viewer's physical space, mirroring the conceptual weight of mortality and architectural decay explored in the work."

The interplay of heavy impasto and cinematic lighting requires a large format to maximize the 'weight' and presence of each piece.

Editorial Artist Statement

The Litany of Lost Hours

"My work operates in the space between traditional fine art concepts and contemporary digital practice, bridging classical chiaroscuro lighting with dark, heavy impasto textures."

Based in Toronto with a background in film, my practice investigates architectural decay, gothic iconography, and the physical weight of time—creating immersive visual narratives that explore existential transition and mortality.
