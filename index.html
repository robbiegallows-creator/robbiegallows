Tactile & Hybrid Workflow

Where Physical Meets Digital

"Every canvas starts with a digital foundation, printed onto canvas before being heavily worked over with physical oil paints and Mod Podge to construct tactile, dimensional textures."

This hands-on impasto technique creates a tangible surface depth that grounds the atmospheric and dark, chiaroscuro themes of each piece in the physical realm.
