Artist Statement

Robbie Gallows creates contemporary gothic expressionist works exploring mortality, mythology, spirituality, memory, consciousness, and the human condition.

Through traditional painting, multimedia, and cinematic storytelling, each work investigates the space between the visible world and the unknown.

The studio practice combines symbolism, texture, found materials, and emotional storytelling to create immersive visual experiences.
