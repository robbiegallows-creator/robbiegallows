Robbie Gallows

Master of Dark Expressionism & Digital Art • BFA, Emily Carr University

"My work exists in the silent spaces between memory, myth, and mortality. Drawing deep influence from 19th-century Victorian gothic romanticism and German expressionism, I build layered visual worlds where shadow is not merely the absence of light, but an active, breathing entity."

Operating out of a custom cedar shed art studio, every piece—whether rendered in oil on physical wood panels or developed as a multimedia digital composition—is crafted with uncompromising dedication to depth and atmosphere.
